About me

Technology-minded, practical, and always learning.

My professional background combines ICT training, hands-on technical support, and classroom teaching. I enjoy diagnosing problems, explaining solutions clearly, and helping people use technology safely and effectively.

Early curiosity & foundation. My journey into technology began long before formal training. Growing up in Kenya, I was the person friends and family called when a phone froze, a laptop slowed down, or a printer refused to cooperate. That early troubleshooting—often with limited tools and plenty of trial and error—taught me resourcefulness and deepened my curiosity about how systems work. I completed my primary education at Watuka Primary School (2009–2016), where access to the computer lab was a rare treat that quickly became a passion. I sat my KCPE in 2016 and moved on to St. Adrian Kiongwan Boys High School, where I balanced academics with self-taught exploration of computer hardware. I completed my KCSE in 2020, eager to turn my hobby into a profession.

Formal training & certifications. In May 2021, I enrolled at the Christian Industrial Training Centre (CITC), where I gained structured, hands-on experience in hardware repair, software troubleshooting, networking, and system configuration. The programme gave me a solid foundation in IT support and cybersecurity basics, and it confirmed that I wanted to build a career in this field. That same year, I earned the Cisco Networking Academy IT Essentials certification (September 2021), which broadened my understanding of operating systems, mobile devices, printers, cloud connectivity, and troubleshooting methodologies. These credentials were more than certificates—they gave me a professional vocabulary and a systematic approach to problem-solving.

Internship & real-world experience. From September to December 2021, I interned at Fastown E.A Ltd, where I moved from theory to practice. I installed Windows operating systems, created bootable USB drives, diagnosed desktop and laptop hardware faults, assisted with motherboard-level diagnostics, configured LAN and internet connections, and supported server maintenance including RAID configuration. That experience taught me the importance of patience, documentation, and clear communication—especially when explaining technical issues to non-technical colleagues. It also showed me that a good IT professional never stops learning.

Teaching & giving back. Between January 2023 and December 2025, I pursued a Diploma in Information Communication Technology at Ahmed Shahame Mwidani Technical Training Institute, deepening my knowledge of computer maintenance, operating systems, networking, internet troubleshooting, system security, and practical diagnostics. During this period, I also completed an ICT teaching internship at Shariff Nassir Girls Secondary School (January–March 2026). I prepared and delivered both theoretical and practical ICT lessons to Grade 10 students, guided learners through computer applications, hardware, software, and networking basics, conducted assessments, and helped maintain the ICT laboratory. Teaching was a transformative experience: it forced me to simplify complex concepts, adapt to different learning styles, and lead with empathy. I also emphasised safe and responsible use of technology—a value I carry into every professional role.

Who I am today. I am an ICT professional who bridges technical expertise and human-centred support. I value honesty, reliability, attention to detail, teamwork, time management, and continuous learning. Whether I am troubleshooting a network outage, upgrading hardware, or helping a student understand IP addressing, my goal is the same: to make technology work smoothly and to empower the people who use it. I am currently open to opportunities in ICT support, computer maintenance, networking, technical services, and ICT education—where I can contribute, grow, and make a meaningful difference.
